1. Product Cards with Inventory Status
Clean, card-based design showing each medicine with:
Medicine image thumbnail
Name, generic name, and manufacturer
Price in ₹ with any discount highlighted
Prominent inventory badge (In Stock, Low Stock, Out of Stock)
Stock count (e.g., "43 units available")
2. Inventory Filters and Organization
Smart filter sidebar allowing users to filter by:
Stock status (All, In Stock, Low Stock, Out of Stock)
Medicine type (Generic, Branded, Ayurvedic, etc.)
Price range with a dual-handle slider
Manufacturer
Therapeutic category (Cardiac, Antibiotics, etc.)
Sort options: Alphabetical, Price (low to high), Stock level, Recently updated
3. Quick Actions Row
Prominent action buttons at the top of each product card:
"Update Stock" button
"Set Alert" button (for low stock notifications)
"View Details" button (expands to show more information)
4. Visual Indicators and Stats
Color-coded status indicators:
Green: Good stock levels
Yellow: Low stock (below 20%)
Red: Very low or out of stock
Quick-glance graph showing stock level history over time
Restock prediction based on sales velocity
5. Detailed View
When a product is clicked, open a detailed view showing:

Complete product information
Stock history chart
Batch information (expiry dates, batch numbers)
Alternative medicines (for out-of-stock items)
Vendor contact information for reordering
6. Inventory Dashboard
Summary cards showing:
Total products
Out-of-stock count
Low stock count
Products expiring soon
Category breakdown pie chart
Recent stock changes activity feed
7. Bulk Management Tools
Checkbox selection to perform actions on multiple products
Bulk update options for price, stock levels, or status
CSV import/export functionality for inventory updates
8. Mobile-Friendly Design
Responsive layout that works well on tablets used in stockrooms
Barcode scanner integration for quick lookups
Swipe actions for common inventory tasks
9. Automated Notifications
Alert system that notifies when:
Stock falls below threshold
Items expire within 90 days
Price changes from suppliers
10. Integration Features
Connect with sales data to show velocity and predict stock needs
Vendor portal access for quick reordering
Analytics integration showing correlation between stock levels and sales
